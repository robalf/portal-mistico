The 11/11 Portal of 2026: What It Is, What It Means, and a Simple Ritual for the Day

By Roberto Rodriguez · October 19, 2026 · 4 min read

The 11/11 portal is the name numerology and modern spirituality give to November 11, a day when the number 11 appears twice in the date. In 2026 it falls on a Wednesday. It's considered an especially favorable day for setting intentions, closing cycles, and making wishes, because 11 is a master number associated with intuition and inspiration. Here's where the idea comes from, plus a simple ritual to make the most of it.

Why is 11/11 considered a "portal"?

In numerology, 11 is the first of the master numbers (along with 22 and 33): it isn't reduced to a single digit because it's believed to carry a more intense vibration. It's associated with intuition, inspiration, and spiritual connection.

November 11 brings that number together twice: the 11th day of the 11th month. And if you also glance at the clock at 11:11, the sequence repeats four times. That's why many people experience it as a symbolic "doorway" between what you think and what manifests: a great moment to decide where you want to direct your energy.

The fascination with 11:11 predates the internet, but it went viral on social media, where thousands of people share their wishes at that time every year. Seeing that sequence on the clock is also one of the best-known angel numbers.

What's special about 11/11 in 2026?

If you add up the full date, 11 + 11 + 2026 (2 + 0 + 2 + 6 = 10, and 1 + 0 = 1), the underlying energy is a 1: the number of new beginnings. In 2026, the portal also lands in the home stretch of Mercury retrograde in Scorpio, which turns direct again on November 13, and two days after the new moon in Scorpio on November 9 (you can see all the phases in the November 2026 moon calendar).

The traditional reading of this combination: review before you launch. It's an ideal day to write down intentions and plan, and to take action from the 13th on, when Mercury resumes its direct motion.

A simple ritual for the 11/11 portal

You don't need anything special. Just a sheet of paper, a pen, and, if you like, a candle.

  1. Choose your moment. The most symbolic time is 11:11 (a.m. or p.m.). If you can't, any quiet moment of the day will do.
  2. Tidy your space. A few minutes clearing your table helps "make room" for what's new.
  3. Write 11 intentions. Phrase them in the present tense and in positive terms, as if they were already real: "I have a job that motivates me," instead of "I don't want to stay in this job."
  4. Pick the most important one and read it aloud three times.
  5. Write one thing you want to let go of on a separate piece of paper and tear it up. It's the "closing of a cycle" that goes along with every new beginning.
  6. Keep your list somewhere special and reread it on the 11th of every month to see how you're progressing.

If you use a candle, light it when you begin and blow it out when you finish. Never leave it burning unattended.

What to do (and avoid) that day

  • Do: plan projects, meditate, journal, practice gratitude, pick a healthy habit back up.
  • Better not to: ask for things out of fear or lack, or center your intentions on controlling another person. Tradition says 11/11 amplifies what you think, so it pays to watch your tone.

How do you know which number accompanies you?

The 11/11 portal is the same for everyone, but in numerology each person also has their own numbers, starting with your life path number, which comes from your birth date. If your number is 11 or 2 (the reduction of 11), tradition says this portal speaks to you with special force.

Numerology and rituals are symbolic and meant for entertainment; they do not replace professional advice for important decisions.

Frequently asked questions

What time is the 11/11 portal?

The portal spans all of November 11, but the most symbolic moment is at 11:11, a.m. or p.m., in your local time.

What happens if I don't do the 11/11 ritual?

Nothing. The portal is a symbolic date for reflecting and setting intentions. You can do the same exercise on any other day; what matters is the intention, not the exact date.

Are there other number portals during the year?

Yes. Dates with repeated numbers, like 1/1, 2/2, 10/10, or 12/12, are also considered portals, though 11/11 is the best known because 11 is a master number.
