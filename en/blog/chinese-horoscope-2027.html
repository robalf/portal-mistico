Chinese Horoscope 2027: Year of the Fire Goat (Sheep)

By Roberto Rodriguez · November 23, 2026 · 10 min read

If you were born in January or early February, the animal on a paper place mat is often wrong. The Chinese horoscope for 2027 does not start on January 1. The Year of the Fire Goat (also called Sheep or Ram — they all translate 羊) runs from Saturday, February 6, 2027, to Tuesday, January 25, 2028, according to the Hong Kong Observatory’s lunar-solar conversion tables. Its formal name is 丁未 (Dīng Wèi), the yin Fire stem with the Goat branch. Anyone born from January 1 through February 5, 2027 is still a Fire Horse. The Earth Monkey year begins on January 26, 2028.

In U.S. cities the same date is Lunar New Year: parades, red envelopes, closed offices in some school districts, and family dinners in Chinese, Vietnamese and Korean communities. Vietnamese Tết follows the same new moon. Korean Seollal can fall a day later in some years because of the time zone; in 2027 the new moon is late on February 6 in China, so most popular calendars still mark the 6th as the turn, but you may see February 7 listed for Korea. For your own sign, use the Chinese zodiac calculator rather than the civil year alone. February 6 also brings an annular solar eclipse in Aquarius, already noted in the 2027 Western horoscope: two calendars, one date.

The February cutoff: confirm your sign

Chinese New Year moves between mid-January and mid-February. A birthday on February 3 can belong to two different animals depending on the year. The calculator is the safest check. For Goat years you are likely to care about, these are the start dates:

Someone born on February 3, 1991 is a Horse, not a Goat: that year the turn came on February 15. BaZi, the four-pillars system, sometimes starts the year at Lichun (February 4, 2027, at about 9:46 a.m. in Beijing). For the popular animal-of-the-year reading, February 6 is the date that matters.

Tai Sui in 2027: who should take more care

Tai Sui is the year’s “official star,” tied to the ruling animal. In a Goat year, folk feng shui flags a few tensions. The Ox sits opposite the Goat and takes the direct clash. The Rat is in a “harm” pair with the Goat. The Dog joins Ox and Goat in a punishment trio. People born in a Goat year “occupy” the same seat as Tai Sui (zhi tai sui): a more visible year, not automatically a worse one, but easier to overcommit.

The traditional advice is caution with moves, big contracts and unnecessary fights — not a year spent indoors. Some people wear red, keep a Tai Sui charm, or make a small respectful gesture at the start of the lunar year. Treat that as symbolism. If you want to compare two generations in a family, the Chinese zodiac parent-child compatibility tool places both animals side by side.

Lucky colors, numbers and directions

Fire sits well with red, orange and purple, and wood feeds fire, which is why green is often added. South is the fire direction; southwest belongs to the Wèi branch, the Goat’s. The numbers most often cited for this year are 2, 3 and 7. Use them as a palette — a red envelope, a south-facing corner of a desk — not as a house-wide dress code.

2027 forecasts for the 12 animals

These sketches are symbolic, for entertainment, drawn from Chinese zodiac tradition and the mood of a Fire Goat year. They are not medical, legal or financial advice, and they do not predict illness, death or guaranteed income. The order follows the wheel: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog and Pig.

Rat

The Rat is in a harm pair with the Goat, so the year asks for a lighter touch. In love, sarcasm is expensive; say what you want without wrapping it in a joke. At work, it is a better year for tightening systems than for forcing a sudden overhaul on everyone else. Money, in the traditional reading, is safer when you build a cushion than when you chase a windfall. For symbolic health, the weak spots are nerves and sleep: fewer late screens, more actual gaps in the calendar.

Ox

The Ox clashes with Tai Sui. This is not the year to win every argument. At work, your stamina still matters, but other people’s deadlines may shift; leave margin. In love, stubbornness is the risk: one awkward conversation early beats months of trench warfare. With money, avoid casual signatures and loans you would hate not to recover. In health symbolism, the usual advice is not to carry every load yourself, and to watch posture, neck and rest — as daily hygiene, not as a diagnosis.

Tiger

The Tiger is not in a direct clash, but the year’s Fire can turn the heat up further. In love there is chemistry and friction: a clear plan beats a pride fight. Work favors visible projects and work that takes nerve, as long as it is not improvised at the last hour. Money arrives, traditionally, through work that is properly billed, not through a bet. For health, the hot year does not ask you to go faster; it asks you to pause.

Rabbit

The Rabbit belongs to the triad with Goat and Pig, and is often read as one of the most comfortable signs of 2027. In love, there is room to mend a misunderstanding or to take a step you have postponed. Work rewards tact and alliances, not spectacle. Money does better with a simple budget and a modest investment in what already works than with a loud new venture. In health, the body prefers gentle routines and less self-criticism; a favorable year is not a reason to ignore fatigue.

Dragon

A Goat year asks the Dragon to turn the volume down one notch. At work, you shine if you leave room for other people; Fire already supplies visibility. In love, generosity beats theater. With money, tradition says not to inflate your lifestyle because one month went well. For health, watch shoulder tension and short nights after intense weeks. A realistic calendar is more useful than a mid-year crash.

Snake

The Snake works well in a year that rewards observation. In love, ask a direct question instead of collecting suspicions; Fire tends to reveal what was already there. Work favors analysis, mediation and quiet skill over the big announcement. Money is protected by reviewing small debts and subscriptions before opening a new one. In symbolic health, the usual notes are about digestive rhythm and not postponing routine checkups — caution, not a prediction.

Horse

The Horse is in a six-harmony pair with the Goat, one of the easier readings of the year. In love, shared motion helps: a short trip or a two-person project beats a speech. Work may open a window to change roles or scenery, if you do not leave as a fugitive. Money holds when the effort is steady; the risk is spending to celebrate every small win. For health, choose movement you actually like, and respect minor injuries before they grow.

Goat

It is your year, and it is also the Tai Sui year. That double billing reads as a spotlight: more eyes on you, more sensitivity, more need not to promise yourself everything. In love, ask for what you need without over-apologizing; Fire helps you say it. At work, visible invitations may arrive; pick the ones that deserve your energy. With money, a cushion and fewer “it’s my year” purchases will do more than a splurge. For health, sleep, and do not turn every invitation into an obligation. A year of your own does not have to be a loud year.

Monkey

The Monkey does well in a Goat climate if the wit turns into something finished, not a way out. At work, new ideas have room, provided they get delivered. In love, consistency is more attractive than a constant surprise. Money: the temptation is a shortcut; tradition prefers a realistic side income to a fast play. For health, it is not only the mind that tires — neck and wrists too, if the day is all screen.

Rooster

The Rooster walks into a year less formal than it prefers, and that is the lesson. At work, quality is still your card; Fire also asks for flexibility when the plan moves. In love, drop a notch of critique and add a notch of listening. Money likes clear accounts and dislikes image purchases. In health symbolism, throat, skin and stress from high standards often come up — not as alarms, as habits: water, sleep, fewer late-night arguments.

Dog

The Dog is in the punishment trio, so the year asks you not to turn every difference into a loyalty test. In love, stand your ground without becoming the relationship’s permanent lawyer. At work, reliability is rewarded and over-control is not. Money is safer in a simple plan than in a poorly measured favor. For health, the tradition is blunt: do not carry everyone else’s problems. Emotional fatigue counts. Ask for help when the “I’ll handle it” list gets long.

Pig

The Pig, in triad with Goat and Rabbit, often has a more fertile year for agreements and for ordinary well-being. In love there is warmth and a wish to take care of people; the excess would be saying yes to everything. Work moves through well-kept relationships more than through a single flourish. Money holds if it is not dissolved in a chain of treats. For health, pleasure at the table is part of your style: the idea is not deprivation, it is not using food as the only comfort in a year that actually gives you room to feel better.

How to use this guide

Read your animal, and maybe your partner’s or your parents’, and keep one idea, not twelve. If you are in tension with Tai Sui, translate that into two or three practical cautions: do not sign blind, do not fight for pride, sleep. If the year looks favorable, do not treat it as a green light to overextend. To compare two people in a family, use the parent-child compatibility tool. For the Western sky of 2027 — Jupiter, Saturn, the August eclipse — see the 2027 horoscope. And if you are planning December 31, the civil new year is covered in New Year’s rituals 2027 around the world; the Chinese year, as above, turns on February 6.

The Lantern Festival on February 20, 2027 closes the two weeks of celebration, not the year itself. The animal remains the Goat until January 25, 2028.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Year of the Fire Goat 2027 start?

Saturday, February 6, 2027, according to the Hong Kong Observatory conversion tables. It ends on Tuesday, January 25, 2028. January 1, 2027 still belongs to the Year of the Fire Horse.

Are Goat, Sheep and Ram the same sign?

Yes. They all translate the same Chinese character, 羊. This site uses Goat, as in the Chinese zodiac calculator.

What if I was born in January or early February?

Your animal may still be the previous year’s. The Chinese zodiac does not change on January 1; it changes at Lunar New Year. The safest check is the calculator, using your full birth date.

Which animals conflict with Tai Sui in 2027?

In folk tradition, the Ox (clash), the Rat (harm) and the Dog (punishment). People born in a Goat year “sit” in the same seat as Tai Sui: a more visible year, not automatically a worse one.

Will Goat natives have a difficult year?

It is not a sentence. It reads as a spotlight: more visibility and more need not to overcommit. Tradition asks for caution with big contracts and unnecessary fights, not for staying still.

How do I know my Chinese zodiac animal?

Use the Chinese zodiac calculator, which accounts for the January and February cutoff. The Goat-year table in this article helps, but it does not replace the exact New Year date of your birth year.
