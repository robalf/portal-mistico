2027 Horoscope: Predictions for the 12 Signs and the Year's Key Dates

By Roberto Rodriguez · October 19, 2026 · 6 min read

The 2027 horoscope is shaped by three major movements: Jupiter in Leo until July 26 and then in Virgo, Saturn in Aries all year, and the total solar eclipse on August 2, one of the longest of the century. In numerology, 2027 is also a universal year 11 (2 + 0 + 2 + 7), a master number associated with intuition. Below you'll find the key dates and what traditional astrology anticipates for each sign.

What does the great eclipse of August 2, 2027 mean?

The total solar eclipse of August 2, 2027 will be visible as total in southern Spain, North Africa and the Middle East, and it ranks among the longest of the 21st century: at its peak, totality lasts more than six minutes, according to NASA calculations. In astrology it falls in Leo, so it is linked to themes of identity, leadership, creativity and self-love, especially for Leo, Aquarius, Taurus and Scorpio.

The slow planets: the backdrop of the year

  • Jupiter, the planet of expansion, moves through Leo until July 26 (joy, creativity, the spotlight) and then through Virgo (order, health, work well done).
  • Saturn stays in Aries: it asks for discipline with projects that are just getting started.
  • Uranus continues in Gemini: changes in communication, technology and the way we learn.
  • Neptune stays in Aries and Pluto in Aquarius: slow, collective transformations.

2027 horoscope predictions, sign by sign

Read your sun sign and, if you know it, your Rising sign too. It often describes better how you will experience the year's changes.

♈ Aries

Saturn and Neptune stay in your sign all year: this is a season of maturity, where steady effort is rewarded over haste. Through July, Jupiter in Leo favors love, creativity and children; from July 26, the focus shifts to daily work and health. Watch for Saturn retrograde starting August 9: a good time to review your commitments before signing new ones.

♉ Taurus

With Jupiter in Leo until late July, your energy centers on home and family: moves, renovations or reunions. From July 26, Jupiter in Virgo, another earth sign, brings you a lighter second half of the year, with good news in love and creative projects. Uranus in Gemini keeps shaking up your finances, so it pays to keep a savings cushion.

♊ Gemini

Uranus in your sign all year asks you to reinvent yourself: your image, your direction or the way you think. The first half, with Jupiter in Leo, is ideal for studying, short trips and communicating. From July, Jupiter in Virgo turns toward your home and family. Be patient with Mercury retrograde from June 10 to July 4, which ends in your sign: double-check before closing any deals.

♋ Cancer

The year kicks off with Jupiter in Leo activating your money and your self-esteem: a great time to charge what you're worth. From July 26, Jupiter in Virgo favors study, travel and the conversations that open doors for you. Mercury retrograde starting June 10 begins in your sign: avoid impulsive decisions during those weeks.

♌ Leo

This is your year. Jupiter is in your sign until July 26, a stage astrology associates with luck, visibility and fresh starts. The great total solar eclipse of August 2 falls in Leo and marks a before and after in your identity or your direction. Mars retrograde from January 10 to April 1 ends in your sign: don't force things, prepare.

♍ Virgo

The first half of the year is about preparation and inner rest. Everything changes on July 26, when Jupiter enters your sign for the first time in about twelve years: personal growth, opportunities and the urge to start fresh. Mars retrograde begins in Virgo on January 10, so the first quarter calls for patience with your energy and your projects.

♎ Libra

Jupiter in Leo through July fills your calendar with social plans: friendships, groups and team projects. From July 26, the pace slows and it's wise to focus on inner work. Saturn in Aries, your opposite sign, keeps testing your relationships: what's solid gets stronger and what isn't becomes clear. Mercury retrograde ends in your sign on October 28.

♏ Scorpio

The first half of the year, with Jupiter in Leo, boosts your career and your reputation: possible promotions or recognition. From July 26, Jupiter in Virgo favors friendships and useful connections. Mercury retrograde starting October 7 begins in your sign: review your paperwork and take nothing for granted during those weeks.

♐ Sagittarius

Jupiter, your ruling planet, spends the first half of the year in Leo, another fire sign: travel, studies and a thirst for adventure. From July 26, the focus shifts to your professional life. The total eclipse of August 2 in Leo may awaken a strong desire to broaden your horizons.

♑ Capricorn

Through July, Jupiter in Leo touches on shared resources, debts and investments: a good time to get your finances in order. From July 26, Jupiter in Virgo, another earth sign, opens you up to travel, study and growth. Saturn, your ruler, in Aries asks you to look after the foundations of your home.

♒ Aquarius

Pluto stays in your sign all year, transforming you deeply. The annular solar eclipse of February 6 falls in Aquarius and opens a new personal chapter, and the total eclipse of August 2, in Leo, your opposite sign, stirs up your relationships. The first half, with Jupiter in Leo, favors partnerships and romance.

♓ Pisces

The year begins with Mercury retrograde in your sign (February 9 to March 3): ideal for reviewing plans before launching them. Jupiter in Leo through July favors health and routine, and from July 26 it enters Virgo, your opposite sign: good news for love, romance and partners.

2027 in numerology: a master year 11

Adding up the digits of the year, 2 + 0 + 2 + 7 = 11, a master number. Tradition links it with intuition, inspiration and shifts in consciousness. To find out how it affects you, calculate your personal year by adding your birth day and month to 2027.

Horoscope predictions are general and are meant for entertainment and reflection. They are not a substitute for professional advice on health, finances or major decisions.

Frequently asked questions

Which signs are luckiest in 2027?

According to astrological tradition, Leo (Jupiter in its sign until July) and Virgo (Jupiter enters its sign on July 26) are the most favored. Sagittarius, Taurus, Capricorn and Pisces also receive good aspects from Jupiter.

When is Mercury retrograde in 2027?

Three times: February 9 to March 3, June 10 to July 4, and October 7 to 28.

Where will the total eclipse of August 2, 2027 be visible?

The path of totality crosses the Strait of Gibraltar, southern Spain, North Africa and the Middle East. In many other parts of Europe and Africa it will appear as a partial eclipse.
