November 2026 Horoscope for All 12 Signs: Scorpio, New Moon, and Supermoon

By Roberto Rodriguez · October 29, 2026 · 11 min read

November arrives loaded with astrology: it begins with the Sun in Scorpio, brings a new moon in Scorpio on the 9th, ends the retrograde season with Mercury direct on the 13th and Venus direct on the 14th, opens Sagittarius season on the 22nd and wraps up with a full Supermoon in Gemini on the 24th. Here's your overview of the month and a reading for every sign: love, work and a practical tip.

The overall mood of the month

November opens with an introspective energy. The Sun travels through Scorpio, the sign of deep emotions, secrets and transformation, alongside a retrograde Mercury and a retrograde Venus: tradition links them with reviewing, reconciling and revisiting unfinished business, especially in relationships and money. That's why the first half of the month is better for reflecting and getting organized than for launching new things or signing major commitments.

From the 13th and 14th on, with Mercury and Venus direct, the atmosphere clears. The second half shifts the tone: the Sun in Sagittarius brings optimism and a desire to plan, and the Supermoon on the 24th in Gemini invites you to talk things through, clear the air and celebrate. Jupiter stays in Leo all month, an influence traditionally tied to expansion, generosity and luck, while Mars leaves Leo for Virgo late in the month and pushes things toward the practical.

Retrogrades: what to do until the 13th and 14th

  • Double-check messages, contracts and payments before sending or signing them.
  • Back up your files and your phone.
  • If someone from your past resurfaces, listen calmly: tradition reads it as a chance to close a chapter or reconcile, not as an obligation to go back.
  • Avoid impulsive decisions about love or money until Venus turns direct again.

How to read your November horoscope

Each reading is written for your sun sign, but if you know your Rising sign, it's worth reading that sign too: it describes how you experience transits day to day. November 2026 horoscope by sign

♈ Aries (March 21 – April 19)

November activates your 8th house, the house of shared finances, intimacy and deep transformation. With the Sun and the November 9 new moon in Scorpio, this is a month to face head-on what you share with others: debts, agreements, buried feelings.

💞 Love: Venus retrograde in Libra, your partnership zone, brings unfinished conversations until the 14th. With Mars in Leo (your house of romance) until the 26th, passion and your appetite for fun are on the rise.

💼 Work and money: Mercury retrograde in your 8th house until the 13th calls for reviewing payments, loans and insurance before you sign anything.

📅 Key date: November 9, new moon: ideal for deciding what you want to transform.

✨ Tip: Don't close any major financial deals before the 14th; review them twice.

♉ Taurus (April 20 – May 20)

The Sun and the November 9 new moon fall in your 7th house, the house of partnership, business partners, and contracts. This is a month about relationships: who stands beside you and on what terms.

💞 Love: Your ruler, Venus, is retrograde in Libra (your 6th house) until the 14th: review your self-care habits and routines. When it turns direct again, you regain a lighter mood and a desire to enjoy time with your partner.

💼 Work and money: The November 24 full moon in Gemini lights up your house of money: a good time to settle accounts and take stock of your income.

📅 Key date: November 14, Venus resumes direct motion.

✨ Tip: Renew an agreement with someone important, but do it clearly and in writing.

♊ Gemini (May 21 – June 20)

Your month begins in the 6th house: daily work, health, and routines. Mercury, your ruler, is retrograde in Scorpio until the 13th, so unfinished tasks and organizational slip-ups come to light so you can fix them.

💞 Love: Venus retrograde in Libra (your 5th house) may bring reunions or memories of an old love until the 14th. After that, romance becomes clearer.

💼 Work and money: Get your schedule and your health in order before the 22nd, when the Sun moves into your 7th house, the house of partnership and business partners.

📅 Key date: November 24: the full moon falls in your sign, a long-awaited personal closing.

✨ Tip: Set aside an afternoon to finish what's pending: you'll feel lighter for your full moon.

♋ Cancer (June 21 – July 22)

Scorpio, your fellow water sign, fires up your 5th house: romance, creativity, children, and leisure. The November 9 new moon is an invitation to start something that excites you: a creative project, a date, a hobby.

💞 Love: Mercury and Venus retrograde may bring back an ex or a forgotten project until mid-month. Look at the memory fondly, but decide from the present.

💼 Work and money: Mars in Leo, your house of money, until the 26th: watch out for impulsive spending.

📅 Key date: November 24, full moon in your 12th house: emotional closure and rest.

✨ Tip: Spend time on what brings you joy without guilt; your energy needs it.

♌ Leo (July 23 – August 22)

The Sun and the November 9 new moon light up your 4th house: home, family, and roots. During the days of Day of the Dead and Samhain, honoring your ancestors and tidying your space feels especially meaningful.

💞 Love: Mars moves through your sign until the 26th and Jupiter is still in Leo: you have energy, charisma, and luck, though Mars can make you impatient. Talk things through before reacting.

💼 Work and money: Venus retrograde in your 3rd house (until the 14th) asks you to review how you speak and what you say in your close circle.

📅 Key date: November 22, the Sun enters Sagittarius and activates your house of romance and creativity.

✨ Tip: Make your home a refuge: small updates or a good tidy-up will recharge you.

♍ Virgo (August 23 – September 22)

Scorpio season activates your 3rd house: communication, paperwork, studies, and short trips. Mercury, your ruler, is retrograde there until the 13th: confirm facts, schedules, and messages before sending them.

💞 Love: Venus retrograde in your house of money and values invites you to revisit what truly matters to you.

💼 Work and money: Mars enters your sign on November 26: a surge of action and organization arrives, perfect for wrapping up the year.

📅 Key date: November 13, Mercury goes direct again.

✨ Tip: Wait until after the 13th to send proposals or sign documents.

♎ Libra (September 23 – October 22)

The Sun and the November 9 new moon fall in your 2nd house: money, values, and self-esteem. This is the month to ask yourself what you're worth and how you spend your energy and your money.

💞 Love: Your ruler, Venus, is retrograde in your own sign until the 14th: you're rethinking your image, your relationships, and what you want to attract. It's not the time for drastic makeovers or impulsive decisions in love.

💼 Work and money: Mercury retrograde in your house of money: review your subscriptions and automatic payments.

📅 Key date: November 14, Venus direct: you feel more like yourself.

✨ Tip: Make a simple budget and jot down three things you value about yourself.

♏ Scorpio (October 23 – November 21)

It's your season: the Sun and the new moon in your sign on the 9th offer you the best moment of the year to set personal goals, like a second New Year. Mercury retrograde in your sign until the 13th asks you to review how you express yourself and what image you project.

💞 Love: Venus retrograde in your 12th house brings buried emotions to the surface until the 14th. It's a good time to heal, not to force things.

💼 Work and money: Mars in Leo (your career house) pushes you to stand out professionally until the 26th.

📅 Key date: November 9: your birthday new moon.

✨ Tip: Write down three goals for your coming year of life and a first step for each one.

♐ Sagittarius (November 22 – December 21)

Before your astrological birthday, the Sun crosses your 12th house: rest, retreat, and closing a chapter. Mercury retrograde in that house until the 13th brings up memories and past matters that need to be put to rest.

💞 Love: The November 24 full moon in Gemini, your opposite sign, lights up your partnership life: a moment of clarity in an important relationship.

💼 Work and money: Mars in Leo, your 9th house, encourages you to study, travel, or bet on a big idea.

📅 Key date: November 22: the Sun enters your sign and your season begins.

✨ Tip: Rest and let go of what has already run its course; your rebirth arrives on the 22nd.

♑ Capricorn (December 22 – January 19)

The Sun and the November 9 new moon activate your 11th house: friendships, groups, and collective projects. It's a great month to bring allies on board for your 2027 goals.

💞 Love: Venus retrograde in your 10th house (the career house) asks you to review your reputation and professional goals until the 14th.

💼 Work and money: Mars in Leo in your 8th house brings movement in shared finances: be careful with agreements and debts.

📅 Key date: November 22: the Sun moves into your 12th house and you close out the year in a more inward, reflective mood.

✨ Tip: Talk to someone you admire about your plans: one conversation could open a door for you.

♒ Aquarius (January 20 – February 18)

The Sun and the November 9 new moon light up your 10th house: career, reputation, and professional goals. It's an ideal month to map out your work direction for 2027.

💞 Love: Mercury retrograde in your 10th house until the 13th asks you to review proposals, emails, and meetings before taking any important steps.

💼 Work and money: Mars in Leo, your 7th house, intensifies partnerships and business relationships: passion, but also possible friction.

📅 Key date: November 24: the full moon activates your house of romance and creativity.

✨ Tip: Share your project with someone who can support it, but wait until the 14th to close deals.

♓ Pisces (February 19 – March 20)

Scorpio season activates your 9th house: studies, travel, philosophy, and beliefs. The November 9 new moon is perfect for planning a course, a trip, or broadening your outlook.

💞 Love: Venus retrograde in your 8th house brings up themes of intimacy and shared finances until the 14th. Speak clearly about what you feel and what you need.

💼 Work and money: Mercury retrograde in your 9th house: review your travel documents and study deadlines.

📅 Key date: November 22: the Sun moves into your 10th house and puts the spotlight on your career.

✨ Tip: Pick one idea that inspires you and take the first concrete step.

On the 9th, at the new moon, write down your intentions for the final stretch of the year. On the 11th, tap into the energy of the 11/11 portal. Frequently asked questions

When is the November 2026 new moon?

On November 9, 2026, in Scorpio. It's an intense new moon, associated with transformation and deep intentions.

When does Mercury retrograde end in November 2026?

Mercury resumes direct motion on November 13, 2026. The so-called post-retrograde shadow lasts until November 30.

When does Venus retrograde end?

Venus turns direct again on November 14, 2026, after beginning its retrograde in October.

Which signs are luckiest in November 2026?

None of them has luck guaranteed. Scorpio takes center stage with the November 9 new moon and Sagittarius kicks off its season on the 22nd, but each sign experiences the month in a different area of life: check the reading for your sign and your Rising sign.

What is the Supermoon on November 24?

It's a full moon that coincides with perigee, the point in the Moon's orbit closest to Earth, so it looks slightly bigger and brighter. In 2026 it falls in Gemini and is traditionally known as the Beaver Moon.
