How to Cleanse and Protect Your Aura Energy

By Roberto Rodriguez · September 20, 2026 · 8 min read

Just as the physical body gets tired and needs rest, the auric field can also become saturated with dense energy built up after intense days, charged environments, or prolonged contact with other people's emotions. Cleansing it regularly helps maintain greater clarity and vitality.

Cleansing with sacred smoke

Using plants such as white sage, palo santo, or natural incense is one of the most widespread energy-cleansing practices. Light the plant, let the flame die down until it only gives off smoke, and gently pass it around your body, paying special attention to your head, chest, and back.

Protective Light Visualization

Close your eyes and visualize a sphere of white or golden light surrounding your whole body, a few inches outside your skin. Imagine this light filtering out any dense energy that doesn't belong to you and strengthening your own field. Practicing it before leaving the house on particularly demanding days can make a noticeable difference.

The power of water

A mindful shower, taking a few seconds to imagine the water washing away any heavy energy you've accumulated, is one of the simplest and most accessible forms of daily energy cleansing. Adding a handful of sea salt to your bathwater boosts this effect for those looking for something deeper.

Protect your energy consciously

Besides cleansing, it's key to learn to set energetic boundaries: spend less time with people who consistently drain you, take conscious breaks in highly charged environments, and prioritize moments of silence and nature so your own field can restore itself naturally.

Protective crystals: which to choose and how to use them

Certain stones are traditionally associated with energetic protection. Black tourmaline is perhaps the most recommended for those who feel especially sensitive to dense environments, since it is said to absorb negative energy before it reaches your field. Obsidian serves a similar function, while amethyst is used more to raise your overall vibration than to protect specifically. Carrying a small stone in your pocket, or placing it near your front door, are the most common ways to incorporate them into your daily protection routine.

Energy protection before big social events

Parties, tense family gatherings, or very demanding work environments are the situations where it's most recommended to "shield" your energy beforehand. Besides visualizing protective light, some people practice setting a brief spoken intention before going in ("I only allow in what does me good") as a conscious reminder to keep your boundaries active throughout the whole event, instead of passively absorbing the general atmosphere.

Signs your energy urgently needs a cleansing

Unexplained fatigue with no clear physical cause, irritability that appears "out of nowhere," trouble sleeping after being in certain environments, or the feeling of "carrying" another person's distress after an intense conversation are common signs that your energy field is saturated. None of these signs replaces a medical consultation if they persist, but many people report noticeable relief when combining these energetic practices with their usual physical and emotional well-being routines.

No. The energy of the aura has not been scientifically proven; it is an esoteric and spiritual belief. If it helps you feel calm, it can be a complementary resource.
