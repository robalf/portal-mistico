Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the Major Arcana and the Minor Arcana in tarot?

The Major Arcana are 22 cards that represent big lessons, life stages, and archetypes, from The Fool to The World. The Minor Arcana focus on everyday situations and are divided into four suits. In a reading, the Major Arcana usually point to underlying themes and the Minor Arcana to day-to-day nuances.

Can I read tarot for myself, or is it better to have someone else do it?

You can read for yourself, and it's a good way to learn, especially with simple spreads like the three-card spread. The important thing is to ask a clear question and write down what you feel. If the topic affects you deeply on an emotional level, it can be hard to stay objective, and a second perspective may help.

Why should I know my rising sign if I already know my sun sign?

Your sun sign describes your essence, while the rising sign is associated with the way you present yourself to the world and how others perceive you when they meet you. That's why two people with the same sign can seem very different. To calculate it, you need your exact date, time, and place of birth.

What does it mean when Mercury is retrograde, and should I avoid signing contracts or making decisions?

In astrology, Mercury retrograde is a period when the planet appears to move backward as seen from Earth, and it is associated with delays, misunderstandings, and reviewing pending matters. It isn't a prohibition: tradition advises rereading things calmly, confirming details, and leaving some margin, rather than putting everything on hold.

How do I know if my tarot deck is the right one, and how should I care for it?

The right deck is the one you're drawn to for its images and find easy to interpret; many people start with a deck with classic symbolism. To take care of it, store it in a clean, dry place, wrapped up or in a box, and use it with respect. Some people add energy-cleansing rituals, but they're optional.
