Signs You Might Be Reliving a Past Life

By Roberto Rodriguez · September 20, 2026 · 9 min read

The belief in past lives holds that the soul passes through multiple incarnations, and that certain patterns, fears, or inexplicable connections in the present life may originate in experiences from previous existences. Although it's a matter of personal faith more than scientific evidence, many people find meaning and relief in exploring it.

Fears or phobias with no apparent cause

An intense fear of something you've never directly experienced in this life — water, heights, a specific type of situation — is, according to esotericism, one of the most common signs of a traumatic experience carried over from a previous life.

Intense, recurring déjà vu

We all experience the occasional déjà vu, but when the feeling of "having already lived this" is particularly intense, detailed, and repeats in similar contexts, some traditions interpret it as an echo of past-life memories resurfacing in the present.

Unexplained attraction to specific cultures or eras

Feeling a disproportionate fascination with a historical period or a culture to which you have no direct family connection — and feeling "at home" when learning about it — is another sign frequently associated with past lives lived in that context.

Instant connections with certain people

Meeting someone and feeling an immediate familiarity, as if you'd already been together for years, is one of the experiences that the concept of "soulmates" or "kindred souls" attributes to the reunion of bonds forged in earlier incarnations.

Exploring without becoming obsessed

Regardless of your level of belief in the topic, reflecting on these patterns can be a valuable self-knowledge exercise — the goal is not to find definitive answers, but to open questions that illuminate your present.

Talents you "already knew how to do" without having learned them

Another sign frequently cited by those who believe in reincarnation is feeling an unusual ease with a specific skill from the very first time you try it — playing an instrument, speaking a foreign language, cooking a particular technique, or even finding your way around a city you've never visited. When that ease isn't explained by your family environment or prior practice in this life, some traditions interpret it as a skill "inherited" from a previous incarnation, stored in the soul's memory rather than in conscious memory.

Recurring dreams with settings you don't recognize

Repeatedly dreaming of the same place, the same historical era, or even the same "identity" different from your own, with a level of detail that surprises you on waking, is another experience many people associate with past-life memories resurfacing during sleep. Unlike an ordinary dream that is quickly forgotten, these dreams tend to feel unusually vivid and leave a persistent emotional feeling throughout the following day.

Birthmarks and the link to past traumas

Some esoteric currents — and certain anthropological studies on reincarnation beliefs across different cultures — point to a curious association between unusual birthmarks and wounds or trauma from a previous life at the same spot on the body. It is a belief widespread in traditions such as the Hindu one or some Indigenous communities, rather than a scientific claim, but it remains one of the most fascinating stories in the popular imagination about reincarnation.

Frequently asked questions

What are the most common signs of past lives?

The most frequently mentioned in the esoteric realm are intense déjà vu, an immediate connection with certain people or places, attraction to eras or cultures you don't know, fears with no clear origin, and recurring dreams. At Portal Místico we treat them as symbolic clues for personal reflection, not as conclusive proof.

Does a déjà vu mean I lived a previous life?

Not necessarily. From the esoteric view, déjà vu can be interpreted as an echo of a previous experience, but it also has everyday explanations, such as momentary memory glitches. I suggest jotting down when it happens and what you felt; those patterns can help you reflect with tools like tarot or numerology.

Is there scientific evidence for past lives?

No, there is no scientific evidence confirming past lives. It is a spiritual belief present in different traditions.

Can recurring dreams be memories of past lives?

According to the esoteric view, a recurring dream with scenes from other eras or unfamiliar places could be a symbolic memory of another life. It can also reflect current emotions. My recommendation is to write down each dream on waking and compare it with what you're living today; afterward you can use tarot as a symbolic aid.

Can an unexplained fear or phobia come from another life?

In esotericism it is proposed that certain fears with no apparent cause, such as of water or heights, could be rooted in experiences from other lives. Even so, they usually have explanations in this life. If a fear limits your day-to-day, the prudent thing is to discuss it with a health professional, in addition to exploring it spiritually.
