Contact

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Have a question, suggestion, comment, or a request about your data? Write to us and we'll be glad to reply.

Email: elportalmistico87@outlook.com

We usually reply within a few days. You can also leave a message in the comment box at the bottom of the home page and of our blog articles.

What can you write to us about?

  • Questions about a tool: if a result isn't clear, tell us what you entered and what you got, and we'll explain how it's calculated.
  • Website bugs: if a link, page, or tool doesn't work on your phone or browser, tell us what device you're using and what happened so we can fix it.
  • Ideas and new topics: new articles and tools largely come from what readers ask for. If something's missing, suggest it.
  • Content corrections: if you find a date, an astronomical fact, or a meaning you think is wrong, let us know and we'll review it.
  • Privacy and data: to exercise your rights or ask about cookies and data, write to us and put the topic in the subject line.

Who replies

Portal Místico is created and maintained by Roberto Rodriguez, who answers the messages that arrive by email. If you write to us, a person replies, not an automated system.

Before you write

Your question may already be answered in the FAQ section of each tool, or on our blog, where we explain in detail how tarot, numerology, the birth chart, and the moon calendar work.
