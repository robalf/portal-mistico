    Frequently asked questions

    What's the difference between a three-card tarot spread and a daily reading?

    The card of the day offers brief, focused guidance, useful as a daily reflection. The past, present, and future spread uses three cards to show how things unfold: what influenced you before, what's happening now, and what trend is opening up. It's a better fit when you want to understand a process or a specific situation.

    What does it mean when a tarot card comes up reversed?

    A reversed card usually suggests that its energy is blocked, delayed, turned inward, or overdone, depending on the tradition. It isn't automatically negative. It's best read alongside the rest of the spread and the question you asked, since each school of tarot interprets it a little differently.

    Why does my horoscope change depending on whether I read it by my sun sign or my rising sign?

    Your sun sign depends on your birth date and reflects your general essence. Your rising sign requires the exact time and place of birth, and speaks to how you present yourself and handle everyday life. That's why the predictions can differ. Many readers check both for a fuller picture.

    What kind of palm photo works best for reading the lines in palmistry?

    Use a sharp photo in good natural light, with your hand open and relaxed, fingers slightly apart, and the palm centered, with no shadows or glare. That makes the heart, head, life, and fate lines easier to tell apart. It's usually best to photograph your dominant hand.
