Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

At Portal Místico (elportalmistico.com) we respect your privacy. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit the site, what it is used for, and what options you have.

1. Site Operator

Portal Místico is an independent website operated from Kentucky, United States. For any privacy-related inquiry, you can write to us at elportalmistico87@outlook.com.

2. You Do Not Need to Create an Account

Portal Místico has no user registration or accounts. The data you enter into the tools (for example, your name, your date of birth, your sign, or the account of a dream) is processed directly in your browser to generate the result and is not sent to or stored on our servers.

3. Information Collected

  • Browsing data (Google Analytics): we use Google Analytics to learn, on a statistical basis, how many people visit the site, which pages they view, from what type of device, and from what approximate country. Google Analytics uses cookies and does not provide us with your name or email.
  • Advertising (Google AdSense): the site displays Google ads. See section 4.
  • Comments: if you write in a comment box, the text you write and the address of the page from which you submit it are forwarded to us by email through the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co). We ask that you not include sensitive personal data in your comments. On blog articles, your comment is also saved in your own browser so that you can view it.
  • Email: if you write to us directly, we will use your address only to reply to you.
  • Browser local storage: some tools save small preferences on your own device (for example, the control of the daily spin of the wheel or whether sound is turned on). That information stays in your browser, and you can delete it by clearing the site data.

4. Google Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense to display ads. Google and other third parties may place and read cookies in your browser, or use web beacons and IP addresses, to collect information as a result of ads being served on this site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to show you ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings, or opt out of other providers' cookies at aboutads.info. How Google uses data when you use our partners' sites or apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

If you visit the site from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, your consent will be requested before advertising cookies are used.

5. Amazon Affiliate Links

As an Amazon Associate, Portal Místico earns from qualifying purchases. When you click a product link, you are taken to the Amazon site, which may use its own cookies to record the purchase. What you do on Amazon is governed by Amazon's privacy policy. We do not receive your personal or payment data.

6. Third-Party Services

The site loads resources from Google Fonts, Google Analytics, Google AdSense, FormSubmit, and Amazon. Each has its own privacy policy. The site is hosted on Netlify, which may log basic technical data (such as the IP address) for security reasons.

7. Minors

Portal Místico is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a minor has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any information you have sent us (for example, a comment or an email). Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Union or in California), the laws may give you additional rights. Write to us at elportalmistico87@outlook.com. We do not sell your personal data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The date of the last update appears above.
