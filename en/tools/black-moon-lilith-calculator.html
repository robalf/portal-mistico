Black Moon Lilith Calculator: Find Your Lilith

Black Moon Lilith is the farthest point of the Moon’s orbit. In astrology it stands for your untamed side: what you won’t let anyone impose on you, your unfiltered desires and your shadow. Enter your birth date to see its exact sign and degree and your Lilith returns.

What is Black Moon Lilith?

The Moon's orbit isn't a circle but an ellipse, with the Earth at one of its foci. The point of the orbit closest to Earth is perigee, and the farthest is apogee. In astrology, the direction of the lunar apogee projected onto the zodiac is called Lilith or the Black Moon. It isn't something you can see in the sky, but a calculated point, just like the lunar nodes or the rising sign.

Its name comes from the Lilith of medieval Jewish tradition, Adam's first partner, who in the story refused to submit and left Eden. Hence its astrological meaning: the part of you that rebels, that won't be tamed and that you may have learned to hide because it didn't fit what was expected of you.

What it represents in your chart

  • Your radical autonomy: the areas where you can't stand being controlled or told how to be.
  • Your unfiltered desire: what truly attracts you, often linked to sexuality and pleasure, beyond the rules.
  • Your shadow: what you repress for fear of rejection and which, if unacknowledged, can come out as anger, jealousy or a sense of being left out.
  • Your power: once integrated, Lilith brings authenticity, magnetism and the ability to say "no" without guilt.

The sign of your Lilith describes the style of that energy. In Aries, a refusal to be held back; in Taurus, an intense relationship with pleasure and possession; in Gemini, sharp words and taboo ideas; in Cancer, conflicts around care and dependence. In Leo it demands recognition without asking permission; in Virgo it hides behind high standards and perfectionism; in Libra it questions pacts and imposed harmony; in Scorpio it lives emotional and sexual intensity with no half measures. In Sagittarius it rebels against dogma; in Capricorn, against authority and hierarchy; in Aquarius, against any group norm; and in Pisces it moves between total surrender and escape. Mean Lilith and true Lilith

There are several "Liliths," and it's worth not mixing them up. This tool calculates mean Lilith, the one most used in astrology: the average position of the lunar apogee, worked out with Jean Meeus's formulas. Its motion is regular and direct, and it goes all the way round the zodiac in about 8.85 years, so it spends roughly nine months in each sign.

True or osculating Lilith follows the actual apogee at each moment. Because the Sun's pull constantly distorts the Moon's orbit, that point swings a lot, up to about 30 degrees from the mean, which is why many astrologers consider it unreliable for interpretation. There's also an asteroid named Lilith (number 1181) and a supposed second moon, "Waldemath's Dark Moon," which has never been confirmed. If you compare with other sites, check that they use mean Lilith.

We've checked the calculation against a reference date: for 12:00 UTC on January 1, 2000, mean Lilith is at 23° 21′ Sagittarius, the same value professional ephemerides give.

Do I need my birth time?

For the sign, almost never. Mean Lilith moves about 6.7 arcminutes a day, so it only changes sign roughly every nine months. If you were born very close to a change, the tool will tell you. You do need your birth time to know which house of your chart it falls in, which shows the area of life where it shows up; you can see it in the birth chart.

Lilith returns

Every 8.85 years, Lilith comes back to the degree it occupied when you were born. These Lilith returns, around ages 9, 18, 27, 35, 44 and 53, are associated with moments when what's been repressed asks for room: changes of image, breaks with family rules, desires you no longer want to keep quiet. For example, for someone born on May 14, 1990, with Lilith at about 21° Scorpio, the returns came in March 1999, January 2008 and November 2016.

From September 2026 to June 2027, Lilith is moving through Capricorn, which in the collective reading puts the spotlight on rebellion against power structures and authority.

How to work with your Lilith

Read the description for your sign and ask yourself where you've kept quiet to fit in. Write down three situations in which you felt angry about having to adapt: they usually point to your Lilith's energy. The goal isn't to turn it into constant rebellion, but to recognise it and give it an outlet: a clear boundary, a desire spoken aloud, a space of your own. To complete the emotional picture, compare it with your moon sign, which describes what you need to feel safe, while Lilith describes what you're not willing to negotiate.

Frequently asked questions

Is Lilith a planet?

No. It is a calculated point: the direction of the Moon’s apogee, the point of its orbit farthest from Earth.

How often does Lilith change sign?

Mean Lilith spends about nine months in each sign and travels the whole zodiac in about 8.85 years.

Why does another site give me a different sign?

It probably uses true or osculating Lilith, which can differ from the mean by up to about 30 degrees, or the asteroid Lilith. This tool uses mean Lilith, the most common choice.

Is Lilith negative?

No. It represents what is repressed and untamed. It can show up as conflict when ignored, but once integrated it brings authenticity and personal strength.

Do I need my birth time?

For the sign, almost never. For the house it falls in within your chart, yes.
