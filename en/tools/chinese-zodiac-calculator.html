Chinese Zodiac: What's My Animal?

Enter your birth year and find out which of the 12 Chinese zodiac animals is yours: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog or Pig.

By Roberto Rodriguez · Updated November 10, 2026

How Does the Chinese Zodiac Work?

Unlike the Western zodiac, which changes signs every month, the Chinese zodiac assigns one animal to each year in a twelve-year cycle. Each animal has its own character and, according to tradition, shapes the personality of people born in its year.

Heads Up If You Were Born in January or February

The Chinese year begins with Lunar New Year, which falls between January 21 and February 20. If you were born before that date, your animal is the one from the previous year. For example, someone born on January 10, 1990 is a Snake (1989), not a Horse.

How Your Animal Is Calculated

The Chinese zodiac runs on a twelve-year cycle, each year with its own animal: Rat, Ox, Tiger, Rabbit, Dragon, Snake, Horse, Goat, Monkey, Rooster, Dog and Pig. The tool looks up your birth year in that cycle. If you were born in January or the first days of February, remember that the Chinese year begins with the new moon between January 21 and February 20, so your animal may be the one from the previous year.

The Five Elements and the 60-Year Cycle

Each animal pairs with one of the five elements—Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal and Water—forming a full sixty-year cycle. That's why a Wood Dragon doesn't get the same reading as a Fire Dragon. The element adds nuance to character: Wood brings growth and creativity, Fire passion, Earth stability, Metal determination and Water adaptability.

The Four Affinity "Triangles"

Tradition groups the animals into four trios that share a nature and tend to get along well: Rat, Dragon and Monkey (wit and ambition); Ox, Snake and Rooster (perseverance and method); Tiger, Horse and Dog (idealism and loyalty); Rabbit, Goat and Pig (sensitivity and harmony). The Twelve Animals of the Chinese Zodiac

Here are the twelve animals with their symbolic character and the years that belong to each. Remember that the Chinese year begins with Chinese New Year, between January 21 and February 20, so if you were born in January or February, check your exact date with the calculator.

Rat — Sensitive Wit

You see paths where others see walls. In love, you show care through practical gestures and lively conversations; your challenge is to trust without trying to anticipate everything. Years of birth: 1948, 1960, 1972, 1984, 1996, 2008, 2020, 2032.

Ox — Serene Strength

You move forward with patience and build bonds that withstand every season. You love through steady actions; when you let a little spontaneity in, your tenderness shines even brighter. Years of birth: 1949, 1961, 1973, 1985, 1997, 2009, 2021.

Tiger — Brave Heart

Your energy opens doors and defends what you love. You need excitement and honesty; your real magic appears when you share the spotlight without dimming your fire. Years of birth: 1950, 1962, 1974, 1986, 1998, 2010, 2022.

Rabbit — Diplomacy of the Soul

You pick up on subtle nuances and know how to create beautiful havens. You look for kind affection and loyalty; setting clear boundaries protects the sensitivity that gives so much good to the world. Years of birth: 1951, 1963, 1975, 1987, 1999, 2011, 2023.

Dragon — Creative Brilliance

You have presence, ambition, and an imagination that can move others. In love, you inspire; remembering that you can ask for support too makes your bonds more genuine. Years of birth: 1952, 1964, 1976, 1988, 2000, 2012, 2024.

Snake — Deep Intuition

You read silences and choose carefully whom to let into your world. Your affection is magnetic and loyal; sharing your doubts keeps mystery from turning into distance. Years of birth: 1953, 1965, 1977, 1989, 2001, 2013, 2025.

Horse — Free Spirit

You need movement, fresh air, and horizons of your own. You love with infectious joy; commitment blossoms when it feels like a journey you chose, not a fence. Years of birth: 1954, 1966, 1978, 1990, 2002, 2014, 2026.

Goat — Artistic Tenderness

Your sensitivity turns everyday moments into something special. You offer support, beauty, and a listening ear; you also deserve to be cared for without having to hint at it. Years of birth: 1955, 1967, 1979, 1991, 2003, 2015, 2027.

Monkey — Inventive Spark

Curiosity and humor are your compass. You win hearts with your quick mind and joy; showing what you feel behind the wit creates a surprisingly deep intimacy. Years of birth: 1956, 1968, 1980, 1992, 2004, 2016, 2028.

Rooster — Radiant Clarity

You observe, organize, and say what others avoid. Your love is attentive and proud; when you soften your standards, your words become warm guidance. Years of birth: 1957, 1969, 1981, 1993, 2005, 2017, 2029.

Dog — Protective Loyalty

Justice and trust anchor your heart. You truly show up in hard times; allowing yourself to lean on others completes your generous way of loving. Years of birth: 1958, 1970, 1982, 1994, 2006, 2018, 2030.

Pig — Luminous Generosity

You enjoy life sincerely and share without keeping score. Your heart creates emotional abundance; choosing calmly whom you give so much to protects your sweetness without hardening it. Years of birth: 1959, 1971, 1983, 1995, 2007, 2019, 2031.
