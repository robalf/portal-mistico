Today's moon phase

See how the Moon looks today: its phase, its illumination percentage, the sign it's in, and how long until the next full moon and the next new moon.

By Roberto Rodriguez · Updated October 26, 2026

The Eight Phases of the Moon

New moon, waxing crescent, first quarter, waxing gibbous, full moon, waning gibbous, last quarter, and waning crescent. The full cycle lasts about 29.5 days. In esoteric tradition, the waxing Moon invites you to begin and the waning Moon to let go.

Why Does the Moon's Sign Matter?

The Moon changes signs every two and a half days and, according to astrology, colors the collective mood. If you want to know which sign the Moon was in when you were born, calculate your moon sign.

What You'll See in This Tool

The tool calculates the Moon's phase at the moment you open the page from the mean lunar cycle: its phase, the percentage of its surface that is illuminated, the zodiac sign it is transiting, and the days left until the next new and full moons. The calculations run in your browser and update every time you visit. Because it uses the mean cycle, the exact moment of a phase can differ by a few hours from astronomical tables.

How Each Phase Influences You, According to Tradition

  • New moon: a time to plant intentions and begin.
  • Waxing crescent: a time to build and take concrete steps.
  • First quarter: time to overcome obstacles and make decisions.
  • Waxing gibbous: refine and perfect.
  • Full moon: culmination, clarity, and gratitude.
  • Waning gibbous: share and pass on what you've learned.
  • Last quarter: review and release what no longer serves you.
  • Balsamic moon: rest and prepare for the new cycle.

The Moon's Sign Matters

The Moon rules the emotions, and the sign it transits colors the mood of the day: in Aries energy is activated, in Taurus enjoyment, in Gemini conversation, in Cancer all things home and family, and so on.
