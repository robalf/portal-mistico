How to get the most from your daily horoscope

Read your sign's message in the morning and hold on to one idea: a tip about love, work or energy. A daily horoscope works better as a little compass for your attention than as an exact prediction. If the text mentions caution at work, for example, you can spend a few extra minutes reviewing your tasks; if it talks about emotional openness, it may be a good day for a conversation you've been putting off.

What to read besides your Sun sign

Your Sun sign describes the essentials of your personality, but the day's sky interacts with your whole chart. That's why it's worth reading the horoscope for your rising sign (how others see you and how you approach the day) and your Moon sign (your emotions) too. Find both with the rising sign tool and the Moon sign tool. If you want to know which planets are influencing your personal chart today, check today's transits.
