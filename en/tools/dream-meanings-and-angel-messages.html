The Oracle: dreams, intuition, rituals, and angelic messages

The Mystic Oracle brings together seven tools for exploring symbols, bonds, and perception. You can interpret your dreams, test your intuition, discover simple rituals, compare signs, learn about the compatibility between parents and children according to the Chinese horoscope, and spin the wheel of angelic messages.

The seven tools

  • Dream meanings: write your dream in your own words. The dictionary recognizes more than 500 symbols, detects several at once—even with conjugated verbs, like "I was flying" or "I was running"—and builds a reading of the whole that connects the symbols with each other, with a question for reflection.
  • Extrasensory perception: a five-round game to test your intuition, with your score and your best streak.
  • Spells and rituals: simple, symbolic practices to focus your intention. Each one includes its intention and the steps to follow.
  • Sign compatibility: what two signs have in common, where they may clash, and how they can complement each other.
  • Parents and children: the compatibility between generations according to the animals of the Chinese horoscope.
  • Angelic messages: a wheel with the nine angelic choirs that you can spin once a day to receive a message.
  • Your guardian angel: enter your date of birth and find out which of the 72 angels of the Kabbalah protects you.

How to interpret your dreams

Write down the dream as soon as you wake up, because details are forgotten quickly. Include places, people, colors, and, above all, how you felt. The tool takes into account colors, qualities (for example, whether something was huge or terrifying), and your role in the dream: chasing is not the same as being chased. The interpretations combine the tradition of dream dictionaries with ideas from Jungian psychology.

The most common dreams—falling, flying, losing your teeth, being late, or being unable to move—often reflect everyday worries. Rather than looking for a prediction, use them as a clue to what your mind is processing.

The nine angelic choirs

Christian tradition describes nine choirs of angels: Seraphim, Cherubim, Thrones, Dominions, Virtues, Powers, Principalities, Archangels, and Angels. On the wheel, each choir has twenty different messages with a reflection for your day. You can spin it once every 24 hours.
