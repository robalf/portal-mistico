2026-2027 Esoteric Calendar

The whole sky and the symbolic dates from November 2026 to December 2027, month by month: new and full moons, supermoons, eclipses, retrograde planets, numerology portals like 11/11 and 12/12, meteor showers and more. Pick a month, tap a day and download the events to your calendar.

What You’ll Find in This Calendar

A guide to what happens in the sky each month and which symbolic dates are coming up. It includes the phases of the Moon (new, full and quarters, with the sign they fall in), supermoons, eclipses, retrograde planets, the Sun’s entry into each sign, equinoxes and solstices, meteor showers and numerology portals.

Key Dates for the Rest of 2026 and for 2027

  • Consecutive supermoons: November and December 2026 and January 2027.
  • Total solar eclipse of August 2, 2027, visible across southern Spain and North Africa. Before it, the annular solar eclipse of February 6.
  • Mercury retrograde in 2027: February 9 to March 3, June 10 to July 4, and October 7 to 28 (dates in UTC; see the Mercury retrograde guide).
  • Numerology portals: 11/11, 12/12 and 1/1. In numerology, 2027 is a universal year 11.
  • Meteor showers: Quadrantids, Perseids, Geminids and more, with the Moon phase at each peak.

How to use it

Tap a month in the top bar or use the arrows. The filters let you see only what interests you (for example, only moons or only retrogrades). Tapping a day opens its events, and below you have the full list for the month. With the “Add to my calendar” button you can take the events to Google Calendar, iPhone or Outlook.

To find out how each event affects you personally, try the birth chart, the horoscope and the numerology calculator. You can also read the 2027 horoscope for the 12 signs.

Astrology, numerology and rituals are symbolic and for entertainment. The astronomical dates are real calculations; their meanings are tradition, not science.

Frequently asked questions

What events does the esoteric calendar include?

New, full and quarter moons, supermoons, solar and lunar eclipses, planetary retrogrades and stations, the Sun and slow planets entering each sign, equinoxes and solstices, numerology portals such as 11/11 or 12/12, meteor showers and traditional festivals.

Where do the dates come from?

Moon phases, eclipses, retrogrades and sign changes are calculated with astronomy-engine, an open-source astronomy library. Meteor shower dates are based on the International Meteor Organization (IMO) calendar. Times are shown in your time zone.

How do I add these events to my phone or Google Calendar?

Press the “Add to my calendar (.ics)” button. A file with the events you have switched on in the filters is downloaded, and you can open it with Google Calendar, the iPhone calendar or Outlook.

Why can an event’s date be different in my country?

Astronomical events happen at a specific instant in universal time (UTC). Depending on your time zone, that instant can fall on the previous or the next day. That is why the calendar times are converted to your zone automatically.

What is a numerology portal?

It is the popular name for dates with a repeated number, such as 11/11 or 12/12. It is a tradition of numerology and modern spirituality: they are considered symbolic moments to set intentions. It has no scientific basis.
