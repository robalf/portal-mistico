What are the 72 angels of the Kabbalah?

In the Kabbalistic tradition, the 72 angels come from the Shem HaMephorash, the "explicit name" of God, made up of 72 three-letter names derived from three verses of the Book of Exodus. Each name is associated with an angel, and tradition divides the days of the year among them, about five days for each.

Why are there lists with different dates?

There are several versions depending on the school: some begin the cycle on March 20 and others on the 21st, and some add hours of the day. Here we use the list most widely used in Spanish-language media, which runs from March 20 (Vehuiah) to March 19 (Mumiah). If you were born on the border between two angels, you can read the previous or the next one as well.

How are the angels grouped?

They are divided into nine choirs of eight angels: Seraphim, Cherubim, Thrones, Dominions, Powers, Virtues, Principalities, Archangels, and Angels. In the The Oracle section you can explore the messages of each choir.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need my birth year?

No. Your guardian angel depends only on the day and month you were born.

Can I ask other angels for help?

Yes. According to tradition, you can invoke any of the 72 angels depending on what you need, although yours accompanies you in a special way.

What is a guardian angel?

In many spiritual traditions, it is a being of light that accompanies and protects each person from birth. In the Kabbalah, it is identified with one of the 72 angels.
