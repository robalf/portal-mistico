Daily, Tomorrow, Weekly and Monthly Horoscope

Cielo Zodiacal is our interactive horoscope for all 12 signs. Pick your sign and read today's, tomorrow's, this week's or this month's forecast, plus a look at the years ahead. Readings are generated from the real date, so they change every day.

What you'll find in Cielo Zodiacal

  • Today's and tomorrow's horoscope: a short message about your mood, love, work and the day's energy.
  • Weekly outlook: the trends for the next seven days, so you can plan with more peace of mind.
  • Monthly horizon: a broader view of the month, with the themes that may take center stage.
  • Your path in 2027, 2028, 2029 and 2030: long-term readings covering the big themes of each year for your sign.

The dates of the 12 signs

Aries (March 21 – April 19), Taurus (April 20 – May 20), Gemini (May 21 – June 20), Cancer (June 21 – July 22), Leo (July 23 – August 22), Virgo (August 23 – September 22), Libra (September 23 – October 22), Scorpio (October 23 – November 21), Sagittarius (November 22 – December 21), Capricorn (December 22 – January 19), Aquarius (January 20 – February 18) and Pisces (February 19 – March 20). If you were born on the cusp between two signs, the exact dates can shift by a day depending on the year.

Sun sign and rising sign

Your daily horoscope is based on your Sun sign, which depends on your birthday. Many people also read the horoscope for their rising sign, which is calculated from your birth time and place and describes how you come across to others. Reading both can give you a fuller picture.

Each sign belongs to an element: fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius) and water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces). The element helps explain why certain signs react in similar ways to the same situation.

Frequently asked questions

How often does the horoscope change?

Today's horoscope changes every day. The weekly and monthly ones refresh at the start of each week and each month.

Can I read someone else's?

Yes, just choose their sign. It's a fun way to share readings with friends and your partner.

Is it a real prediction?

Astrology has no scientific validity. Our readings are for entertainment and are meant to inspire reflection.
