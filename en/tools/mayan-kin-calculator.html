Mayan Kin Calculator: Your Tzolkin Seal and Tone

Your Mayan kin is the combination of one of the 20 seals and one of the 13 tones of the sacred 260-day calendar. Enter your birth date to see your Dreamspell kin, your oracle (guide, analog, antipode and occult) and your day in the traditional Maya count.

What is a Mayan kin?

The Maya peoples of Mesoamerica used several calendars at once. One of the most important was the Tzolk'in, a sacred cycle of 260 days that combines 13 numbers with 20 day signs. Each day in the cycle is unique and is called a kin, which in Yucatec Maya means "day" and also "sun." Depending on the day you were born, you have a kin that tradition links with your character and your mission.

The Tzolk'in didn't disappear with the Spanish conquest: Maya spiritual guides in Guatemala, the ajq'ij or daykeepers, have kept count of the days without interruption for centuries, and in many K'iche' communities it is still consulted for ceremonies, births and important decisions.

Two different counts: traditional and Dreamspell

If you look up your kin online, you may find different results. That's because there are two systems, and this calculator shows you both:

  • Traditional Maya count: the one kept by Maya daykeepers and used by archaeologists, aligned with the GMT correlation (number 584283) between the Maya calendar and ours. In it, December 21, 2012, the end of the 13th b'ak'tun of the Long Count, was the day 4 Ajaw. The signs are named in Mayan languages, such as Imox, Iq' or Aq'ab'al.
  • Dreamspell: a modern system created by José and Lloydine Argüelles in 1990, inspired by the Tzolk'in. It uses names such as "Red Dragon" or "White Wizard," starts its year on July 26 and skips February 29, so it doesn't line up with the traditional count. It's the version that has become popular worldwide and on social media.

Neither is "the right one" for everything: the traditional count is the historical one, still alive among the Maya; Dreamspell is a contemporary system with its own symbolic language. We've checked both against known dates: December 21, 2012 gives 4 Ajaw in the traditional count and kin 207 in Dreamspell, and July 26, 2013 gives kin 164, the Yellow Galactic Seed.

The 20 seals and the 13 tones

In Dreamspell, your kin is made of a solar seal and a galactic tone. The seals describe your essence and fall into four colours that repeat in order: red initiates, white refines, blue transforms and yellow ripens. So the Red Dragon speaks of birth and nurturing; the White Wind, of spirit and communication; the Blue Night, of dreams and abundance; and the Yellow Seed, of flowering and goals.

The tones show how that essence is expressed, from 1 to 13: Magnetic (unifying purpose), Lunar (recognising the challenge), Electric (activating and serving), Self-Existing (giving form), Overtone (empowering), Rhythmic (balancing), Resonant (channelling), Galactic (harmonising), Solar (realising intention), Planetary (manifesting), Spectral (releasing), Crystal (cooperating) and Cosmic (transcending). For example, someone born on May 14, 1990 is kin 16, Yellow Electric Warrior: the Warrior's intelligence and fearlessness, put at the service of others.

Your oracle: guide, analog, antipode and occult

Dreamspell completes each kin with four other seals that form a cross, called the destiny oracle:

  • Guide: the energy that steers and teaches you. It shares your seal's colour.
  • Analog: your support and natural complement, the energy you flow with.
  • Antipode: your challenge, what confronts you and makes you grow.
  • Occult: your hidden strength, what appears in difficult moments. Its kin and yours always add up to 261.

Reading the five seals together gives a much richer picture than your seal alone.

The wavespell and the year

The 260 kins are arranged in 20 wavespells of 13 days, each opened by a magnetic tone. The wavespell you were born in colours your kin with the theme of its first seal. Dreamspell also celebrates the "Day Out of Time" on July 25 and starts the new year on July 26, a date that matches the heliacal rising of Sirius at the latitude of the Maya lands.

How to use your kin

Start with the keywords for your seal and tone and think about when in your life you recognise them. Then look at your oracle and find people close to you whose kin matches your analog or antipode: it's a fun way to understand affinities and friction. If you're interested in other calendars that give you a symbol by birth date, compare your kin with your Chinese zodiac animal and your life path number.

Frequently asked questions

What is a kin in the Maya calendar?

It is each of the 260 days of the Tzolk’in, made by combining one of the 13 numbers or tones with one of the 20 signs or seals.

Why is my kin different on other sites?

Because there are two counts: the traditional Maya count and José Argüelles’s Dreamspell, which don’t match. This calculator shows you both.

What if I was born on February 29?

Dreamspell doesn’t count February 29, so it shares a kin with February 28. In the traditional count it has its own day.

What is the occult kin?

It is one of the four seals of your oracle and represents your hidden strength. Your kin and your occult kin always add up to 261.

Do I need my birth time?

No. Your kin depends only on the date.
