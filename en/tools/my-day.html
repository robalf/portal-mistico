What is “My Day”?

It is your daily summary on Portal Místico. With just your birth date it calculates the position of your Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter and Saturn and compares it with today’s sky. From that come your energy and love indexes, the transits being activated and a short reading of the day. If you have already calculated your full birth chart on this device, the page uses it to add the house the Moon is crossing.

Where do the number and color of the day come from?

The number is your personal day in Pythagorean numerology: add your birth day and month to the current year (personal year), add today’s month and day, and reduce to one digit. The color and ruling planet follow the traditional planetary days: Sunday (Sun), Monday (Moon), Tuesday (Mars), Wednesday (Mercury), Thursday (Jupiter), Friday (Venus) and Saturday (Saturn). To go deeper, try the life path number calculator.

And the ritual and the countdown?

The ritual is the same one you see in the Lunar Compass: it depends on today’s moon phase. The countdown uses the dates of the esoteric calendar: upcoming new and full moons, eclipses, retrogrades, solstices and meteor showers.

What is the streak?

It counts the consecutive days you open Portal Místico on this device. If you skip a day, it starts again. It is just a game to build the habit of taking a moment for yourself each day.

Frequently asked questions

Do I have to enter my date every day?

No. It is remembered only on your device; when you return, your day appears right away. You can change it any time with the “Change my details” button.

Do you store my data?

We do not send it to any server: your date and streak are kept only in your browser’s storage. If you clear your browser data, they are lost.

Why does it not ask for my birth time?

To keep it quick. Without the time, the natal Moon and houses cannot be calculated well, so we use only planets that do not change sign within a day. For a house-based reading use the full birth chart.

Are the energy and love indexes a prediction?

No. They are a symbolic 1–10 index calculated from the day’s aspects; use them for inspiration and reflection, not as a prediction.
