Personality by your name

Enter your full name and discover the vibration it expresses in numerology: the way you present yourself, relate to others and create.

By Roberto Rodriguez · Updated November 10, 2026

The expression number

In numerology, adding up all the letters of your full name gives your expression number. While your life path number speaks to your journey, your expression number describes your natural talents and how you show them to the world.

Which name should I use?

Tradition uses the full name on your birth certificate. Some people also try the name they use day to day to see how its energy differs. We explain it in name numerology.

Which number is calculated

When you enter your full name, you get your expression number (also called the destiny number): the sum of all the letters reduced to a single digit. It describes your natural talents, the way you show up in the world and the kind of contribution you tend to make. Each letter has a value from 1 to 9 based on the Pythagorean chart, the most widely used in Western numerology.

Expression, soul urge and outer personality

Numerology finds three readings within a single name. Adding up all the letters gives the expression number; the vowels give the soul urge number, which speaks to what you truly desire deep down; and the consonants give the outer personality number, the image you project. If the three vibrations are very different, you may show one thing while wanting another, which is deeply human and worth looking at with kindness.

Which name to use and what to do with the result

Use your full birth name. If you have changed your name or go by a different one in everyday life, run that version too and notice the differences. The result is an invitation to recognize your strengths and work on your weak spots, not a verdict. Also find your life path number from your birth date and read our article on name numerology. Letter value chart

In Pythagorean numerology, each letter is worth a number from 1 to 9, following the alphabet and starting over after 9:

1: A, J, S
2: B, K, T
3: C, L, U
4: D, M, V
5: E, N, W
6: F, O, X
7: G, P, Y
8: H, Q, Z
9: I, R

Accent marks and the letter Ñ are simplified: the tool normalizes them (Ñ counts as N) before adding.

What the vowels reveal: your soul urge

  • Number 1 (vowels): Deep down, you crave independence and setting your own course.
  • Number 2 (vowels): You long for peace, a harmonious relationship and a sense of belonging.
  • Number 3 (vowels): You long to express yourself, create and enjoy life.
  • Number 4 (vowels): You want security, order and to build something lasting.
  • Number 5 (vowels): You long for freedom, adventure and new experiences.
  • Number 6 (vowels): You want a home, love and to care for your loved ones.
  • Number 7 (vowels): You long to understand, to be in silence and to seek the truth.
  • Number 8 (vowels): You want achievement, recognition and financial independence.
  • Number 9 (vowels): You long to serve, contribute and live with purpose.

What the consonants reveal: your outer image

  • Number 1 (consonants): Others see you as determined and confident.
  • Number 2 (consonants): You come across as kind, discreet and easy to get along with.
  • Number 3 (consonants): You give the impression of being friendly, expressive and creative.
  • Number 4 (consonants): You convey seriousness, reliability and order.
  • Number 5 (consonants): You come across as dynamic, curious and attractive.
  • Number 6 (consonants): You convey warmth, trust and responsibility.
  • Number 7 (consonants): You come across as reserved, analytical and mysterious.
  • Number 8 (consonants): You project authority, ambition and capability.
  • Number 9 (consonants): You project generosity, maturity and charisma.

Frequently asked questions

If I change my name, does my number change?

Your birth expression number doesn't change, but the name you use adds its own vibration.

Should I include both last names?

Yes, the traditional reading uses the full name.

How is it different from the life path number?

The life path number comes from your birth date; the expression number comes from the letters in your name.
