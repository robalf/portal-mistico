Online Palmistry: Read the Lines of Your Hand

Mano del Destino is our palmistry tool. It guides you through photographing your palms, looks at the main lines, and offers a clear symbolic interpretation based on the classic traditions of palm reading. The photo is analyzed right inside your own browser.

How It Works

First you prepare both palms: the app helps you check that the photo has good light, little blur, the whole hand inside the frame, and no harsh shadows or glare. Then you review the photo, and the tool describes the characteristics of each line, for example whether it is long, curved, deep, or splits, and explains what that characteristic means according to traditional palmistry.

The app always separates what is observed from what is interpreted, so you know what each message is based on. You can also save your readings and check out the "Learn Palmistry" section if you want to go deeper.

The Main Lines of the Hand

  • Heart line: starts below the pinky finger and is associated with the emotional world and affectionate bonds.
  • Head line: crosses the center of the palm and is related to thinking, decisions, and imagination.
  • Life line: curves around the base of the thumb and symbolizes vitality and major changes. Contrary to popular belief, its length does not indicate how many years you will live.
  • Fate line: it rises through the center toward the middle finger and speaks of direction, goals, and changes of course. Not everyone has it clearly marked.

Which hand do you read?

In tradition, the non-dominant hand shows the potential you were born with, and the dominant hand reflects what you have built through your decisions. That is why the app asks for both palms: comparing them is one of the most interesting parts of the reading. The shape of the hand, the mounts, and the length of the fingers also play a role.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to the internet?

No. The analysis happens on your device, and the images are not sent to our servers.

Do the lines change over time?

Minor lines can change over the years; the main ones tend to stay stable.

Is palmistry a science?

No. It is a traditional, symbolic practice, and our readings are for entertainment purposes.
