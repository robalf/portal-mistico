Which oracle should I choose?

  • For a quick answer: the yes-or-no tarot. One card, one answer.
  • To understand a situation: the runes, with their past, present, and future spread.
  • For deep advice: the I Ching, the Chinese oracle of the 64 hexagrams.
  • For love: synastry compares your chart with your partner's.
  • For your year and your day: the solar return and today's transits.

How do I ask a good question?

Ask a single, clear question about something that depends in part on you. Breathe, concentrate for a few seconds, and choose without overthinking. Avoid asking the same thing many times in a row: the first answer is usually the one that tells you the most.

Frequently asked questions

Are the oracles free?

Yes. All of Portal Místico's oracles are free, and you don't need to sign up.

Are the answers random?

Cards, runes, and coins are chosen at random with your device's secure generator, as in a real spread. Synastry, solar return, and transits are calculated using the actual positions of the planets.

Can I make important decisions with an oracle?

Use them for inspiration and reflection. For health, money, or legal matters, always consult a professional.
