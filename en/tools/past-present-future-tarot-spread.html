Past, Present, and Future Tarot

The classic three-card spread: the first card shows what brought you here, the second where you are now, and the third where the situation is heading.

By Roberto Rodriguez · Updated October 26, 2026

How do you read this spread?

Read the three cards as a story. The past explains the origin, the present shows the current challenge or energy, and the future points to the trend if everything stays the same. The future isn't written: what you do today can change it.

Learn to do it with your own deck

If you have a physical deck, our article on the three-card tarot spread walks you through shuffling, cutting, and reading step by step.

What each position represents

The three-card spread is a classic thanks to its simplicity and narrative clarity. The first card speaks to the past: the causes, background, or decisions that brought you to this situation. The second shows the present: what's happening now, your inner state, and the factors weighing on you. The third points to the future trend if things continue on their current course. The future is never written: the third card indicates a likely direction, not a destiny.

How to read the three cards as a story

The most useful approach is to read them together, like a sentence with a beginning, middle, and end. Notice how they relate: does the past explain the present? Does the third card resolve the tension of the second, or deepen it? Look at the suits and arcana too: if all three are Major Arcana, the theme is especially significant; if Cups dominate, the matter is emotional; if Swords abound, there's a lot of mental activity and perhaps worry.

How to phrase your question well

  • Good: "What do I need to understand about my work situation?"
  • Good: "How can I move forward in my relationship?"
  • Not helpful: "Am I going to get fired?" (closed, creates anxiety and little clarity).

Frequently asked questions

What question should I ask?

An open question about a situation: “What do I need to know about my job?”

How far into the future does it reach?

Usually the next few weeks or months.

Can I repeat the spread?

It's best to wait until the situation has moved forward before asking the same thing again.
