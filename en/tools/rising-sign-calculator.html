What is the rising sign?

Your rising sign (or ascendant) is the zodiac sign that was rising over the eastern horizon at the exact moment you were born. If your Sun describes who you are inside, your rising sign describes your “front door”: the first impression you make, how you react to new things and how you move through the world. We cover it in more detail in what the rising sign is.

Why do I need my exact birth time?

Because of the Earth's rotation, all twelve signs cross the horizon in one day, so your rising sign changes roughly every two hours. A difference of just a few minutes can change it if you were born near a sign change, which is why the calculator warns you when your rising sign falls in its first or last degrees.

What is a ruling planet?

Each sign has a planet that “rules” it: Mars for Aries, Venus for Taurus and Libra, Mercury for Gemini and Virgo, and so on. The ruler of your rising sign is the ruler of your whole chart, and the house it sits in shows where a good part of your energy is headed.

Frequently asked questions

How long does the rising sign take to change?

About two hours on average, although some signs rise faster than others depending on latitude and time of year.

Can I find my rising sign without my birth time?

Not precisely. Without the time, you can only narrow it down to a few possible signs. You can request your birth time from the vital records office or the hospital where you were born.

Is the rising sign more important than the sun sign?

They complement each other: the Sun describes your essence, and the rising sign describes how you express it. To see them together with your Moon and houses, use the full birth chart.
