Saturn Return Calculator: When Is Yours?

Your Saturn return happens when Saturn comes back to the exact spot it occupied when you were born, around ages 29, 58 and 88. Enter your birth date to see the exact day of each return, whether Saturn crosses once or three times and which sign your natal Saturn is in.

All calculations run on your own device. We do not store your data.

By Roberto Rodriguez · Updated November 25, 2026

What is a Saturn return?

Saturn takes about 29.5 years to travel all the way around the zodiac. When it gets back to the exact degree it occupied at the moment of your birth, astrologers call it your Saturn return. It's one of the best-known transits because it coincides with ages many people experience as turning points: the end of youth around 29, full maturity around 58 and old age around 88.

In astrological tradition, Saturn is the planet of time, limits, responsibility and structure. Its return is read as a test of what you've built: whatever stands on solid ground gets stronger, and whatever was held up by inertia asks for change. That's why it's linked with big decisions, such as changing jobs, committing to a partner, moving, having children or closing chapters that no longer fit.

How this tool calculates your return

The calculator finds Saturn's actual position on the day you were born using Astronomy Engine, an open-source astronomy library whose results match NASA's ephemerides (JPL Horizons) to within thousandths of a degree. It then scans the following years and finds every moment when Saturn passes that same degree of ecliptic longitude again.

  • If you don't know your birth time, that's fine: Saturn moves so slowly, about two arcminutes a day, that a difference of a few hours barely changes the result.
  • One pass or three: seen from Earth, Saturn spends about four and a half months a year moving retrograde. If your return coincides with that stretch, it crosses your natal degree three times: direct, retrograde and direct again. The tool shows you every date.
  • The exact age varies from person to person, between 28 and 30 for the first return, because Saturn's orbit is elliptical and its apparent speed changes from sign to sign.

A verified example: for someone born on May 14, 1990, Saturn was at 25° 16′ Capricorn. Their first return came on February 2, 2020, with a single pass. The second will have three passes: March 15, June 29 and December 11, 2049. The third will arrive on January 17, 2079.

The three stages: 29, 58 and 88

  • First return (ages 28-30): the move into adult life. It's common to rethink your career, your relationship and where you live, and to ask which parts of your life you chose and which you inherited from other people's expectations. Many people feel pressure or fatigue in the months before and a new clarity once it's over.
  • Second return (ages 57-60): the review of maturity. It's associated with approaching retirement, becoming a mentor and choosing to spend your time on what really matters.
  • Third return (ages 86-89): a stage of wisdom and closure, where what matters most is your legacy and your relationships with the people around you.

In between come other Saturn transits that tradition also watches: the squares, around ages 7, 22 and 36, and the opposition, around 14-15 and 44. They're moments of partial adjustment within the same cycle of roughly 29 years.

Your return by the sign of your natal Saturn

The sign your Saturn is in shows where the lesson plays out. With Saturn in a fire sign (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius), the return tends to be about confidence and taking charge of your own life. In earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn), about work, money and material foundations. In air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius), about relationships, agreements and the way you think. In water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces), about family, emotional bonds and boundaries.

The house Saturn occupies in your birth chart refines the reading further, and for that you do need your birth time. You can look it up with the free birth chart.

Who is having a Saturn return now

Saturn entered Aries in February 2026 and will stay there until April 2028. So the people having their first return now were born, roughly, between April 1996 and February 1999, when Saturn was also moving through Aries. People born between 1967 and 1969 are going through their second return. If you belong to either group, the calculator will give you the exact day.

How to handle your Saturn return

Saturn rewards honesty and sustained effort, so the best preparation is practical. Review your commitments and keep the ones you truly want to sustain. Write down a goal for the next seven years, roughly the length of each phase of the cycle. Look after your body and your rest, because this stage tends to demand energy. Above all, don't make decisions just out of fear of "being late": the return isn't a deadline but a checkpoint. Frequently asked questions

At what age is the Saturn return?

The first comes between ages 28 and 30, the second between 57 and 60 and the third between 86 and 89. The calculator gives you the exact dates for your birth.

How long does a Saturn return last?

The exact pass over your natal degree is a single moment, but its influence is usually considered active for one to two years, especially if Saturn crosses the same point three times.

Do I need my birth time?

Not for the dates: Saturn moves very slowly, so a few hours make almost no difference. You do need it to know which house of your chart Saturn is in.

Why do some people get three dates?

Because Saturn spends several months a year moving retrograde. If the return falls in that period, it crosses your natal degree three times: direct, retrograde and direct again.

Is a Saturn return a bad thing?

Not necessarily. It’s a stage of review and maturity. It can bring pressure, but also clear decisions and achievements that last.
