Simple rituals and spells

Choose your intention—protection, abundance, or love—and receive a simple ritual using materials you have at home, with the steps explained.

By Roberto Rodriguez · Updated October 26, 2026

Rituals you'll find

  • Protection: the circle of calm, the salt shield, or the protective knot.
  • Abundance: the seed of purpose, the gratitude bowl, or the coin jar.
  • Love: the self-love letter, the harmony ritual, or the red thread.

When should I do a ritual?

Many people choose the new moon to plant intentions and the full moon to give thanks and let go. Check today's phase on the today's moon phase page.

Before you begin a ritual

A simple ritual works as a symbolic act that organizes your attention and your intention. It doesn't need expensive materials: candles, salt, water, paper, and a pen are usually enough. Prepare a tidy space, silence your phone, breathe deeply, and define clearly what you want in a positive, specific sentence (for example, "I want to feel calm at work" instead of "I don't want bad things to happen to me").

Safety first

  • Never leave a candle burning unattended, and place it on a heat-resistant surface, away from curtains and papers.
  • Keep water nearby and put out the candle by gently blowing it out or using a candle snuffer.
  • If you use incense or herbs, ventilate the room; avoid smoke if there are people with asthma, babies, or sensitive pets.
  • Do not use these rituals as a substitute for medical, legal, or psychological care.

What to expect and how to close the ritual

The power of a ritual lies in what changes within you: more clarity, calm, or resolve. When you finish, give thanks, put out the candle, and write in a notebook how you feel and what concrete action you will take in the coming days. A ritual without action is just a wish; with action, it becomes a first step.

Choose the date with intention: the new moon favors planting, the waxing moon building, the full moon giving thanks, and the waning moon letting go. Check today's moon phase, the ritual of the day, and the 11/11 portal ritual. These are symbolic and wellness practices; they don't guarantee external results.

Frequently asked questions

Are rituals dangerous?

No, they are simple, symbolic practices. If you use candles, never leave them burning unattended.

Do I need special materials?

No. They all use everyday items: water, salt, paper, a candle, or a plant.

Do they really work?

They are tools for focusing your intention and your attention. Their power lies in what you do afterward.
