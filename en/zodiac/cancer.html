Cancer: Personality, Love, Career and Compatibility

By Roberto Rodriguez · October 31, 2026 · All signs

Cancer is the sign of home and sensitivity: protective, intuitive and deeply loyal to loved ones. People born under it tend to feel first and think later.

Cancer at a Glance

DatesJune 21 – July 22
ElementWater
ModalityCardinal
Ruling planetThe Moon
SymbolThe crab
Traditional day of the weekMonday
Associated colorSilver or white

Cancer Personality

Cancer is a Water sign with a cardinal modality: it starts things from emotion. It's ruled by the Moon, the celestial body of cycles, memory and intimacy, which is why it's associated with nostalgia, caretaking and an intuition that picks up on the emotional atmosphere before anyone says a word.

Its symbol, the crab, sums up its character: a hard shell on the outside to protect what's tender within. Tradition credits Cancer with empathy, generosity toward its own people and a great talent for creating a safe haven.

Its challenge is retreating. Faced with criticism or fear, Cancer can hide in its shell, hold a grudge or take a neutral comment as an attack. Putting its feelings into words is, according to this reading, its biggest practice.

Strengths

  • Empathy and intuition
  • Family loyalty
  • Ability to care for others
  • Emotional memory

Challenges

  • Hypersensitivity
  • Withdrawal
  • Mood swings
  • Difficulty letting go

Cancer in Love

In love, Cancer is tender, devoted and protective. Cancer looks for a secure bond, an emotional home where it can be vulnerable. It remembers dates, tastes and details, and appreciates being cared for in return.

To keep the relationship flowing, it helps Cancer to speak up when something hurts instead of waiting for the other person to notice, and to tell the difference between caring and controlling.

Cancer at Work and with Money

At work, Cancer stands out in caregiving and support professions: health, education, hospitality, psychology, cooking and any field where the human touch matters. Cancer is also often an excellent manager of day-to-day details.

Cancer does best in warm, stable environments. With money, it's careful and farsighted, especially when family is involved; its challenge is not letting anxiety about security hold back reasonable decisions.

Cancer Compatibility

High affinity: Scorpio, Pisces, Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn.

Medium affinity: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius.

Intense relationship: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius.

This ranking follows the same element-based logic as our compatibility tool. It's a symbolic reading: a real relationship depends on communication, respect, and shared experiences.

A Tip for Cancer

When something hurts, write it down before you react. Putting the emotion into words gives you perspective and helps you ask for what you need.

Cancer FAQ

What dates does Cancer cover?

June 21 to July 22, give or take a day depending on the year. A birth chart with your time and place of birth can confirm your sign if you were born on a cusp.

Why is Cancer associated with the Moon?

Because the Moon, which rules this sign, symbolizes emotions, memory and cycles, themes at the heart of the astrological reading of Cancer.

What element is Cancer?

Water, along with Scorpio and Pisces. It's the element of emotions and intuition.
