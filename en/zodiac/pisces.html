Pisces: Personality, Love, Career and Compatibility

By Roberto Rodriguez · October 31, 2026 · All signs

Pisces is the sign of imagination and compassion: sensitive, intuitive, and dreamy. Those born under it tend to pick up on what others feel and move between reality and their inner world.

Pisces at a Glance

DatesFebruary 19 – March 20
ElementWater
ModalityMutable
Ruling planetNeptune (and Jupiter in traditional astrology)
SymbolTwo Fish
Traditional day of the weekThursday
Associated colorSea green or lilac

Pisces Personality

Pisces is a Water sign with a Mutable modality: it flows and adapts. In modern astrology it's ruled by Neptune, the planet of dreams, inspiration, and the intangible; tradition also assigns it Jupiter. That's why Pisces is associated with art, spirituality, empathy, and imagination.

Its symbol, two fish swimming in opposite directions, points to the duality between the real and the dreamed. Tradition credits Pisces with compassion, creativity, intuition, and a special ability to comfort and understand.

Their challenge is escapism and a lack of boundaries. They can absorb other people's emotions, retreat from problems into fantasy, or let others decide for them. Learning to set boundaries and to anchor themselves in simple routines is, according to this reading, their biggest practice.

Strengths

  • Empathy
  • Imagination
  • Intuition
  • Compassion

Challenges

  • Escapism
  • Lack of boundaries
  • Idealization
  • Absorbing others' emotions

Pisces in Love

In love, Pisces is romantic, tender, and devoted. They idealize their partner, love with an open heart, and look for a connection that's almost spiritual. They sense what the other person needs before it's said.

To keep a relationship healthy, it helps Pisces to see the real person and not just the ideal one, and to express their needs instead of quietly adapting.

Pisces at Work and with Money

At work, Pisces shines in creative and caring fields: music, art, writing, film, psychology, therapy, nursing, education, or social work. They bring sensitivity and vision to a team.

They perform best in calm environments with a sense of purpose. Their challenge is organization and time management; splitting the day into blocks and using reminders helps ground their ideas. With money, Pisces can be overly generous; a simple budget prevents decisions driven by emotional impulse.

Pisces Compatibility

High affinity: Cancer, Scorpio, Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn.

Medium affinity: Gemini, Libra, Aquarius.

Intense relationship: Aries, Leo, Sagittarius.

This ranking follows the same element-based logic as our compatibility tool. It's a symbolic reading: a real relationship depends on communication, respect, and shared experiences.

A Tip for Pisces

Spend ten minutes a day on a concrete routine: tidying up, walking, or cooking. Grounding yourself in the physical protects your energy and feeds your creativity.

Frequently Asked Questions About Pisces

What dates does Pisces cover?

February 19 to March 20, give or take a day depending on the year. A birth chart with your time and place of birth will confirm your sign if you were born on the edge.

Why does Pisces have two fish?

They represent two directions, the real and the dreamed. In astrological terms, they symbolize the duality between the practical and the imaginative.

Which signs are Water signs?

Cancer, Scorpio, and Pisces. They're linked to emotions, intuition, and sensitivity.
